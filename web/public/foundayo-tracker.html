Foundayo vs. the injection apps

Foundayo® (orforglipron) was approved in 2026 as the first oral GLP-1 for weight loss that can be taken any time of day — no empty-stomach window, no injection sites, no weekly shot day. It's a genuinely different routine.

Almost every GLP-1 tracker was built for weekly injections and then adapted. GLPill was built the other way around: for the person who takes a pill every single day and wants the streak, the trend, and the report — without handing their health data to a server. You enter the dose plan your prescriber gave you; GLPill never suggests doses.

Foundayo tracker — questions

Is there an app to track Foundayo (orforglipron)?

Yes. GLPill is built specifically for daily oral GLP-1 pills like Foundayo. Log the pill in one tap, build a streak, track your weight trend and side effects, and generate a doctor-ready summary — around a daily-pill routine, not a weekly shot.

Can Foundayo be taken any time of day?

Foundayo (orforglipron) is the first oral GLP-1 for weight loss that can be taken any time of day, with or without food or water restrictions — unlike Rybelsus, which needs an empty stomach and a 30-minute wait. Always follow the exact instructions your prescriber gave you. GLPill tracks your routine; it doesn't give medical advice.

How is GLPill different from injection tracker apps?

Injection trackers are built around shot days, injection sites and a weekly cadence. Foundayo is a daily pill, so consistency and streaks matter more. GLPill is built around the daily-pill routine.

Is my data private?

Yes. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no tracking. Your data is stored on your iPhone and synced only through your own private iCloud — the App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

Does my history sync to a new phone?

Yes. It syncs privately across your devices through your own iCloud, so it survives a reinstall or a new phone.
