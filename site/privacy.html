GLPill Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 15, 2026  ·  Version 1.1

Summary: GLPill stores everything you enter on your device and syncs it through your own private iCloud, so your history follows you across devices and survives a reinstall. The app has no servers of its own, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We never see, collect, transmit, share, or sell your data. Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple.

1. Who we are

GLPill ("the app", "we", "us") is an iOS application published by Ayush Gupta (the "developer"). Contact: ayushgupta0610@gmail.com. This policy describes how the app handles information when you use it on your Apple device.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the GLPill iOS app and to this website (glpillapp.com). It does not cover Apple's own services (the App Store, Apple ID, StoreKit payments, iCloud device backups, notifications), which are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Important distinction: the sections below about "no collection" describe the iOS app, which stores your data on your device and in your own private iCloud — never on any server of ours. This website is different: like most websites, it uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymized visitor traffic (pages viewed, general location, device type), and if you choose to join the waitlist it stores the email address you submit so we can notify you at launch. See "About this website" below.

3. Data the app stores — and where it lives

To do its job, GLPill saves the information you type into it in a private database on your device, and syncs that database through your own private iCloud (Apple's iCloud, tied to your Apple ID) so your history follows you across your devices and survives reinstalling the app:

There is no sign-up, no email, no phone number, no name, no location, no contacts access, no photo access, and no advertising identifier use anywhere in the app. The iCloud sync uses Apple's private CloudKit database, which only you can access with your Apple ID — GLPill has no ability to read it.

4. Data we collect on our servers

None. GLPill runs no backend servers of its own and has no capability to receive or read your data. Your health data lives on your device and in your personal iCloud, both within Apple's ecosystem and under your control; we never see it. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests that send your personal information to us. Because iCloud sync uses Apple's private database (your data, your Apple ID, not accessible to the developer), our App Store privacy label remains "Data Not Collected".

5. Purchases and subscriptions

GLPill Pro subscriptions are offered, billed, and managed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We receive only an anonymous, device-local confirmation from Apple's StoreKit framework that a subscription is active. We never receive your name, email address, payment details, or Apple ID. Refunds, cancellations, and billing questions are handled by Apple via Apple Support or Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

6. Notifications

Daily pill reminders and the 30-minute meal timer are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's notification framework. They are optional (you can decline or revoke permission in iOS Settings) and no notification content is sent to us or anyone else.

7. Sharing features you control

The app can generate a doctor-visit summary (text) and a progress card (image) that you may choose to share via the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you explicitly do so, and you choose the recipient. The progress card deliberately omits your medication name by default. Once you share content, it is governed by the receiving app or service.

8. iCloud sync and backups

GLPill syncs your data through Apple's private CloudKit database — this is your personal iCloud, tied to your Apple ID, and GLPill has no access to it. This is what lets your dose history, weight trend and settings appear on your other devices and come back if you reinstall the app. It requires that you're signed into iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled; if you're not, the app simply works locally on that device. Separately, your app data may also be included in standard iCloud or encrypted computer backups if you have those enabled. In all cases the data stays within Apple's ecosystem and under your control — we never receive or read it.

9. Security

On your device, your data is protected by iOS's built-in encryption, and GLPill opts its database into Apple's strongest file-protection class (Complete Protection), meaning the file is encrypted whenever your device is locked. In transit and at rest in iCloud, your data is protected by Apple's CloudKit security. Because GLPill has no accounts and no servers of its own, there is no password to steal from us and no GLPill server that could be breached.

10. Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you keep using the app. Because your history now syncs to your private iCloud (so you don't lose it by accident), deleting the app from one device alone does not erase it — that's by design, so reinstalling brings it back. To remove everything permanently: delete the app, then clear its iCloud data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage) › GLPill › Delete Data, and remove any old device backups if you want historical copies gone. There is nothing for us to delete on any server of ours, because your data is never sent to one.

11. Your privacy rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

Laws such as the EU/UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over personal data that companies collect about you — access, correction, deletion, portability, and opting out of sale or sharing. Because GLPill does not collect, receive, sell, share, or process your health data on any server of ours (it stays on your device and in your own iCloud), there is no data held by us to access, correct, delete, or port: you exercise all of these rights directly, on your own device and through your Apple/iCloud account settings. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use your data for targeted advertising. If you believe we hold any personal data about you (for example, because you emailed us), you may contact us at the address below to request access or deletion of that correspondence.

12. Children's privacy

GLPill is intended for adults using prescription GLP-1 medications and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
