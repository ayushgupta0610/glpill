GLPill Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026  ·  Version 1.0

Summary: GLPill stores everything you enter only on your device. The app has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We never see, collect, transmit, share, or sell your data. Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple.

1. Who we are

GLPill ("the app", "we", "us") is an iOS application published by Ayush Gupta (the "developer"). Contact: ayushgupta0610@gmail.com. This policy describes how the app handles information when you use it on your Apple device.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the GLPill iOS app and to this website (glpillapp.com). It does not cover Apple's own services (the App Store, Apple ID, StoreKit payments, iCloud device backups, notifications), which are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Important distinction: the sections below about "no collection" describe the iOS app, which genuinely stores everything only on your device. This website is different: like most websites, it uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymized visitor traffic (pages viewed, general location, device type), and if you choose to join the waitlist it stores the email address you submit so we can notify you at launch. See "About this website" below.

4. Data we collect on our servers

None. GLPill has no backend servers and no capability to receive your data. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests with your personal information. Our App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

5. Purchases and subscriptions

GLPill Pro subscriptions are offered, billed, and managed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We receive only an anonymous, device-local confirmation from Apple's StoreKit framework that a subscription is active. We never receive your name, email address, payment details, or Apple ID. Refunds, cancellations, and billing questions are handled by Apple via Apple Support or Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

6. Notifications

Daily pill reminders and the 30-minute meal timer are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's notification framework. They are optional (you can decline or revoke permission in iOS Settings) and no notification content is sent to us or anyone else.

7. Sharing features you control

The app can generate a doctor-visit summary (text) and a progress card (image) that you may choose to share via the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you explicitly do so, and you choose the recipient. The progress card deliberately omits your medication name by default. Once you share content, it is governed by the receiving app or service.

8. Device backups

GLPill does not sync your data to iCloud and has no cloud database. However, like most iOS apps, the app's local data is included in your device backups if you have iCloud Backup or encrypted computer backups enabled. Those backups are encrypted and controlled entirely by you through your Apple account settings — we have no access to them.

9. Security

Your data is protected by iOS's built-in encryption. In addition, GLPill opts its database into Apple's strongest file-protection class (Complete Protection), meaning the data file is encrypted whenever your device is locked. Because there are no accounts and no servers, there is no password to steal from us and no server breach that could expose your data.

10. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the app. To delete everything permanently, delete the app from your device (and remove old device backups if you want historical copies gone). There is nothing for us to delete on any server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

11. Your privacy rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

Laws such as the EU/UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over personal data that companies collect about you — access, correction, deletion, portability, and opting out of sale or sharing. Because GLPill does not collect, receive, sell, share, or process your personal data on any server, there is no data held by us to access, correct, delete, or port: you exercise all of these rights directly, on your own device. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use your data for targeted advertising. If you believe we hold any personal data about you (for example, because you emailed us), you may contact us at the address below to request access or deletion of that correspondence.

12. Children's privacy

GLPill is intended for adults using prescription GLP-1 medications and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

13. Health disclaimer

GLPill is a tracking tool. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and never suggests doses. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.
