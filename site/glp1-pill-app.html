The GLP-1 pill app — questions

What is a GLP-1 pill?

An oral form of a GLP-1 receptor agonist — taken daily as a tablet instead of a weekly injection. Examples: Foundayo (orforglipron) and Rybelsus (oral semaglutide). GLPill is built to track a daily oral GLP-1 routine.

Is there an app made for oral GLP-1s instead of injections?

Yes. Most trackers were built for weekly injections. GLPill is built around a daily pill: one-tap streaks, a daily reminder, and (for Rybelsus) an automatic 30-minute empty-stomach timer.

Which GLP-1 pills does it support?

Foundayo (orforglipron), Rybelsus (oral semaglutide), and compounded oral GLP-1s. You enter the dose plan your prescriber gave you — GLPill never suggests doses.

Is my data private?

Yes. No accounts, no servers, no analytics. Data stays on your iPhone and syncs only through your own private iCloud — the App Store label is "Data Not Collected."

Does it track weight and side effects too?

Yes — a weight trend with milestones, a side-effect log, and a doctor-ready summary of adherence, doses, weight change and side effects.
