The Rybelsus 30-minute rule, made easy

First thing, empty stomach

Take it after waking, before eating or drinking anything else.

A small sip of water

Swallow the tablet whole with no more than ~4 oz of plain water. Don't split, crush or chew it.

Wait 30 minutes

Wait at least 30 minutes before eating, drinking anything else, or taking other oral medicines. GLPill runs this timer and tells you the exact minute you can eat.

Then have breakfast

After the wait, eat and drink normally. Always follow the exact instructions from your prescriber and pharmacist — GLPill tracks your routine, it doesn't give medical advice.

Rybelsus tracker — questions

Is there an app to track Rybelsus?

Yes. GLPill is built for Rybelsus (oral semaglutide). Log your daily dose in one tap, run the automatic 30-minute empty-stomach timer, track your weight trend and side effects, and build a doctor-ready report.

What is the Rybelsus 30-minute rule?

Rybelsus is taken first thing on an empty stomach with no more than ~4 oz of plain water, and you wait at least 30 minutes before eating, drinking anything else, or taking other oral medicines. GLPill times this for you.

Does GLPill tell me when I can eat?

Yes. When you log your dose, GLPill starts a 30-minute timer and notifies you the moment the empty-stomach window is over.

Is my data private?

Yes. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone and syncs only through your own private iCloud — the App Store label is "Data Not Collected."
