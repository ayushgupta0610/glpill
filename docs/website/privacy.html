GLPill Privacy Policy

Effective: July 12, 2026

The short version

Everything you enter in GLPill stays on your iPhone. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. We never see, collect, transmit, or sell your data.

What data GLPill handles

GLPill stores your medication choice, dose logs, weight entries, side-effect notes, and protein/water counts in a local database on your device. This data never leaves your device through GLPill.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. GLPill receives only an anonymous confirmation that a subscription is active — never your name, email, or payment details.

Notifications

Daily reminders and the 30-minute meal timer are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent anywhere.

Data deletion

Deleting the app permanently deletes all data stored in it.

Health disclaimer

GLPill is a tracking tool, not a medical device and not medical advice. Always follow your prescriber's instructions. GLPill is not affiliated with or endorsed by Eli Lilly and Company or Novo Nordisk A/S.
