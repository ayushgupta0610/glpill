# GLPill Go-To-Market Plan — $5K MRR in 3–5 months

Source playbook: Starter Story interview with Jake, founder of Early (push-up alarm app) — youtube.com/watch?v=uvIcGuN2iO8 (transcript at `transcripts/uvIcGuN2iO8.txt`), cross-referenced with the Pep AI / Quittr / Cal AI research (Notion decision log).

## The playbook, extracted from the video

Jake's numbers: launched end of 2025 → $50K/month in month 4; 200K+ downloads in 2026; $29.99/yr / $9.99/mo with free trial; app built in 19 days; **first influencer video live 7 days after starting marketing**; first $1K revenue 3 weeks after that video; $30K in month 3.

His rules, verbatim-close:

1. **"Focus on top of funnel only."** Building doesn't matter; views are the unknown. Marketing gets priority over everything.
2. **Native fit beats ads.** Find creators doing *day-in-the-life / lifestyle content where the app appears naturally*. The sell to the creator: keep making the videos you already make, but get paid — win-win-win (creator paid, founder gets downloads, viewer gets a solution without watching an ad).
3. **Winning video anatomy:** POV day-in-life → the moment the app naturally appears (his: alarm → push-ups → green check) → no pitch. Conversions happen in comments: someone asks "what's the app?", creator replies and pins it.
4. **Deal structure: 2–4 videos/month per creator with a VIEW GUARANTEE, ~$2–3 CPM** on guaranteed views. Caps downside; you pay for delivered views.
5. **Power law:** most videos do okay; the money is made by the occasional 1–5M-view outlier. You must run enough volume to catch outliers.
6. **Attribution: stay simple.** Track every video + date in a sheet; when a conversion spike lines up with a view spike, that video did it. Accept the black box: "put in 5K, get out 10." You will lose money on individual creators and won't know which.
7. **Mass outreach is a numbers game.** Tons of DMs, few responses — normal. He also used a mass cold-email tool (Instantly) for influencer outreach at scale.
8. Tools he named: Superwall (paywalls), Mixpanel (analytics), Claude Code (building), **Instantly (mass influencer email outreach)**, Cal (bookings), Google Sheets (runs everything), QuickBooks, plus an influencer-payments tool and a UGC-sourcing tool (names unclear in transcript — evaluate Collabstr / Insense / JoinBrands as the equivalent category).

## Target creator profile (whom exactly)

**Tier A — GLP-1 journey micro-creators (the core bet)**
- Who: US women (and some men) 28–55 documenting weight loss on GLP-1s; 10K–100K followers; posting 3+×/week; faces on camera; comment sections asking questions.
- Content types that fit natively: "day in my life on [med]," morning routines, "what I eat in a day (GLP-1)," weekly weigh-in videos, "GLP-1 tips I wish I knew."
- Find them via hashtags: `#glp1`, `#glp1journey`, `#glp1community`, `#ozempicjourney`, `#zepboundjourney`, `#rybelsus`, `#orforglipron`, `#foundayo`, `#weightlossjourney` (filter: recent, US, engagement > 2%).
- Also search TikTok/IG for: "oral GLP-1", "GLP-1 pill", "started Foundayo", "Rybelsus morning routine" — creators talking about PILLS are the highest-intent, smallest, cheapest cohort and exactly our users.

**Tier B — adjacent lifestyle/routine creators**
- Morning-routine, "5–9 before my 9–5," midlife-fitness, over-40 health creators whose audience skews to the GLP-1 demographic. Only after Tier A converts.

**Tier C — nano creators (1K–10K) for volume + UGC**
- Gift a yearly promo code + small flat fee ($25–75/video). Their videos double as ad creative later (get usage rights in the agreement).

**Disqualify:** medical professionals giving clinical advice (compliance optics), giveaway/engagement-bait accounts, anyone whose audience is visibly non-US.

**Deal terms cheat-sheet (Jake's structure adapted):**
- Anchor at **$2–2.5 CPM on guaranteed views** — e.g., a creator averaging 20K views/video: 2 videos + 40K guaranteed views = **$80–100/month**. A 50K-average creator: ~$250/month.
- Nano creators: $25–75 flat/video or free year + affiliate energy.
- Always in writing (simple one-page agreement): deliverables, view guarantee + make-good clause, usage rights for ads ("we may reuse this video in paid ads for 6 months"), FTC disclosure required (#ad or paid-partnership label — non-negotiable, protects both sides), payment on posting via PayPal/Wise.
- Track everything in one Google Sheet: creator, platform, followers, avg views, rate, videos posted (links + dates), views at 24h/7d, spike observed (y/n), next post date.

## Attribution (Jake's spike method + one Apple tool)

1. Every video's post date/time goes in the sheet.
2. Watch App Store Connect → Analytics daily: impressions, downloads, trial starts. A download spike within 24–48h of a video = that video worked.
3. Give each ongoing creator a unique campaign link for their bio: `https://apps.apple.com/app/id<APPID>?ct=<creatorname>&mt=8` — shows up under App Analytics → Sources → Campaigns. (Most installs will still come from search after watching — the spike method is primary.)
4. Weekly review: kill creators with 2 straight no-spike months; double the posting cadence of anyone who spikes.

## Channel stack by phase (sequenced by leverage)

1. **Build the creator list (200 rows).** 2 hrs/day scrolling the hashtags above (IG directly; TikTok via web/VPN since you're in India — or lean on Tier-C UGC marketplaces for TikTok coverage). Log: handle, platform, followers, avg views (eyeball last 10 posts), email if in bio, content style, pill vs injection.
2. **Set up handles**: @glpillapp on IG + TikTok. Bio: "The app for GLP-1 pills 💊 Foundayo · Rybelsus" + App Store link (once live).
3. **Produce 15 faceless reels** with the existing reel-editor pipeline: screen-demo with hand (the SoberTok slideshow style validated by the sobriety-app founder in our research), streak POVs, "things nobody tells you about GLP-1 pills" text-overlay slideshows, progress-card mockups. 5. **Draft 3 Reddit value posts** (r/Semaglutide, r/GLP1, r/Rybelsus-adjacent): "I built a free-to-try tracker specifically for the pills because everything else is injection-first" — honest builder posts do well; check each sub's self-promo rules first; Pep AI's founder got his first 100 users from one Reddit post.

### Phase 1 — Launch week (the moment App Review approves)
1. **Apple Search Ads** exact match, $20–30/day cap: `orforglipron`, `foundayo`, `glp-1 pill`, `rybelsus tracker`, `glp1 tracker`, `oral semaglutide`. These are new keywords with near-zero competition — likely your cheapest installs, and instant paywall data.
2. **Fire the outreach cannon**: 30–50 DMs/day + Instantly sequence to every emailed creator on the list. Target: **10 signed creators in week 1** (expect 5–10% response; the list of 200 exists for this reason).
3. Reddit posts go live (spread over the week, not same day).
4. Organic reels continue daily; every winning organic hook becomes a creator brief variant.
5. **Goal: first creator video live within 7 days of launch** (Jake's cadence). One purchase attributed to a creator video = validated; scale.

### Phase 2 — Weeks 2–6: find the repeatable unit
- Budget: start at **$500–750/month** in creator deals (6–10 micro creators on view guarantees). Reinvest 100% of early revenue back into creators.
- Weekly: review the sheet → cut no-spikes, re-sign spikes at 4 videos/month, raise nano→micro budget as data justifies.
- Watch the funnel in App Store Connect + StoreKit: installs → trial starts → conversions. If trial-start rate <5%, iterate the paywall (copy/ordering, not price, first). If trial→paid <30%, the 3-day trial window may need onboarding nudges (day-2 reminder notification is already built).
- **ASO iteration**: check which search terms drive installs (App Analytics), rework subtitle/keywords once at week 4.

### Phase 3 — Months 2–5: ride the power law to $5K
- Scale creator count toward **20–30 active creators** posting 2–4×/month (Jake's engine). Monthly creator budget ramps with revenue: $1K → $2.5K/month by month 3–4, funded increasingly by MRR.
- **Repurpose the best creator/UGC videos into paid ads** (usage rights already secured): Meta advantage+ and TikTok Spark Ads on the proven hooks only. Start $20/day, kill anything above ~$3 cost-per-trial within a week. Paid ads amplify winners; they don't find them.
- Land **1–2 mid-tier creators (100–500K)** with revenue-funded budgets once micro data proves the conversion story — this is where the 1M+ view outliers live.
- Ship v1.1 (Apple Health import + any top App Store review request) — review velocity and rating (prompt for rating after day-7 streak) compound ASO.
- If orforglipron news moments hit (new dose approvals, insurance news), have creators post same-week — trend-surfing beats evergreen.
