# GLPill — Organic Growth Playbook (adapted from Post Bridge's Growth Guide)

Source: post-bridge.com/growth-guide (jack friks). Synthesized and adapted to GLPill. This runs **before** paid ads. It largely confirms our existing plan (GTM_PLAN.md) but adds a few corrections — flagged ⚠️.

## The core sequence
Video → Profile → Bio → Landing page → Download → Active user. Every step must be optimized. Getting views is step one; converting them is the real job.

## Step 1 — Account creation (Day 1)
- ONE TikTok + ONE Instagram account, brand new. (We have @glpillapp on IG.)
- **Bio must turn views into traffic:** say *what* you offer and *why* they should come, with a direct link. On IG, make it a **Business/Creator account** so you can put the App Store / glpillapp.com link in the bio. On TikTok you can't link on new accounts — just type the URL/app name.

## Step 2 — Warmup (Days 2–8) ⚠️ THE BIG CORRECTION
- **DO NOT POST for 7 days.** New accounts that post immediately get flagged bot-like and land in "0–100 view jail." 3 days can work but 7 is the safe number.
- Daily (~15 min/platform): scroll ONLY GLP-1 / weight-loss-journey content, follow relevant accounts (our creator list is perfect for this), watch full videos, leave real comments. This teaches the algorithm your niche — what you engage with is the first audience your posts get shown to.
- **Save 3–5 viral videos/day** (100K+ views or 1k+ likes) in our niche that we could recreate. These become content raw material.
- ⚠️ **Post from the mobile app for the first ~10 posts** — not web, not a scheduler/API (no Post Bridge yet). The algorithm needs to see a real human. Our AirDrop-to-phone flow is fine; just post natively.
- **Impact on our timeline:** if @glpillapp warmup began ~Jul 13, the safe first-post date is ~Jul 20–21 (day 8), NOT Saturday Jul 18 (~day 5). Recommend holding the first reel to ~day 8. Better a few days late than in view-jail.

## Step 3 — Content-market-fit (Days 8–30)
- Goal: find your **winning format** — content that consistently gets 1,000+ views and comments from actual ICP (GLP-1 pill users).
- **Method:** recreate the viral videos you saved — keep the exact hook/format/audio, swap the context to GLP-1 pills. Copying proven formats beats inventing from scratch. Our original text-slide reels are good, but *mix in recreations of proven-viral GLP-1 formats.*
- **1 post/day, quality over quantity.** One thoughtful post beats 5 sprayed out.
- **CTA discipline:** put the CTA in the caption and the pinned first comment — NOT a hard sell in the video. "Feels like an ad" = no views. Educate/entertain/relate first; mention GLPill naturally (or just show it on screen and let commenters ask "what app is that?").
- **Two metrics that matter:** watch time (hook in first 3 seconds) and comments (spark discussion). Short videos favor both.
- **Patience:** "not allowed to feel discouraged until you've tried 100 formats." The author took 300+ videos to find his winner, now posts 3/day. Reset expectations — the first reels probably won't pop.
- Tools: CapCut (free) to recreate formats; "TikTok/Reel to MP3" to grab the viral audio.

## Riding trends (ongoing)
- Add **1–2 trend posts/week** on top of proven formats (don't replace them). Adapt a trending sound/format to GLP-1 within 24–48h.
- Spot trends early: sounds/formats with 10K–50K uses. A single trend video can lift ALL your other videos (momentum on new accounts is shared).

## Views → customers (ongoing)
- Bio has the direct app/site link; captions state plainly what GLPill does.
- **Reply to every comment early on** — engagement boosts reach, and the more commenters like you, the more they click your profile.
- Make content around **problems GLPill solves** (missing doses, the 30-min wait, protein, doctor visits) and mention the app as the solution — genuinely helpful, not salesy.
- Show, don't tell: screen recordings, before/afters, or the app running in the background of a routine video.
- **Mistakes to avoid:** obvious sales pitches; lazy CTAs ("download my app X"); expecting instant conversions; ignoring comments.

## How this maps to our assets
- Our week-1 + week-2 reels (`marketing/reels/`) = the "originals." Per the guide, ALSO recreate 2–3 proven-viral GLP-1 formats during content-market-fit.
- Our creator list (`docs/creators.csv`) = who to follow/engage during warmup, and later who to pay.
- Once a format wins and the account is trusted (10+ native posts, consistent views), Post Bridge or a scheduler becomes fine for scaling to 2–3/day.
