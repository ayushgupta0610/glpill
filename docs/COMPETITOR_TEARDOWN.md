## ⚠️ Two findings that update our strategy
1. **Shotsy v3.0 (March 2026) already added oral-pill tracking (Foundayo/orforglipron, oral Wegovy) AND a "Maintenance Mode."** Our "pill-first" wedge is now **contested** — the timing window is real but the leader is moving. We still win on being *purpose-built* for pills (name, the Rybelsus 30-min timer, pill-specific UX, streak psychology) vs. Shotsy's bolt-on. But speed matters; don't assume we're uncontested.
2. **MeAgain's top complaints are "only works on 1 device" + "data loss."** That is *exactly* what our CloudKit sync solves. This is a gift — a concrete, credible differentiator we can lead with.

## GLPill's real wedges (each maps to a competitor weakness)
- **Purpose-built for pills** — the daily-pill routine, streaks, the empty-stomach timer. Shotsy = "shots" in the name; pills are an add-on.
- **Syncs across devices + survives reinstall (CloudKit)** — directly answers MeAgain's #1 complaint. "Never lose your history."
- **Private by design** (no servers, on-device + your iCloud) — nobody else emphasizes this; strong for a stigmatized niche.
- **Reliable & simple** — MeAgain is "glitchy / crashes / data loss." A fast, clean, boring-in-a-good-way app is a wedge.
- **Price** — undercut Shotsy's $49.99. Our $39.99/yr looks fair; consider it a talking point ("half the price of the leader").

## What to STEAL (validated by their success)
- **Maintenance Mode** — Shotsy shipped it; demand is proven (and the audience asks for it, e.g. @jasmines.best.life). High-priority backlog item.
- **A shareable/viral widget** — MeAgain's capybara widget became "a social media phenomenon" and drove growth. A tasteful, on-brand Home Screen widget (streak flame + "take pill") that people *want* to screenshot is a real growth engine, not just a feature. Already in `BACKLOG.md` — this raises its priority.
- **Estimated medication-level curve** — Shotsy's premium hook; Glapp's whole pitch. Could be a Pro feature later.

## What to AVOID
- **Feature bloat + instability** (MeAgain). Our reliability is a selling point — don't trade it for a marketplace/AI-everything.
- **Injection-first framing** — stay unmistakably the *pill* app.
