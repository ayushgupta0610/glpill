## GROUP C — Weigh-in units + outlier guard

**Files:** `App/Sources/Features/Progress/WeightEntrySheet.swift`, plus a small pure helper `App/Sources/Core/Logic/WeightOutlier.swift` (TDD).

- **kg/lb toggle:** add a `Picker(.segmented)` (kg | lb) bound to a local `@State metric`, seeded from the passed `metric`. Switching converts the displayed number in place (no data change). Store the current-unit choice: on Save, also write `UserSettings.usesMetric = metric` (heal the global default). (WeightEntrySheet has `@Environment(\.modelContext)`; fetch/update the `UserSettings` singleton on save.)
- **Outlier guard (pure):** `WeightOutlier.isSuspicious(newKg:lastKg:)` → true when `lastKg != nil` and `abs(new-last)/last > 0.25`. On Save, if suspicious, present a confirm alert: "That's a big jump from your last weigh-in (\(formatted last)). Did you mean \(other unit)?" with **Save anyway** / **Cancel**. Only save on confirm.
