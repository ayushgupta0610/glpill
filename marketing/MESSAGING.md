# GLPill — Messaging system (canonical)

Everything public — site hero, IG/YouTube bio, reel endcards, captions, launch posts —
routes through this. Updated 2026-07-20 to the **twist-forward (privacy hook)** lane.

## Why this lane
"The app made for GLP-1 pills" is a *category label*, not a hook — and it's now contested
(Shotsy added pill tracking in March 2026). Our two wedges **no competitor leads with**:
1. **Private by design** — on-device + your own iCloud, no account, no servers, and
   **completely free** (nothing to sell means nothing to harvest). For a stigmatized niche
   this is *emotional*, not a spec.
2. **Pill-native ritual** — the daily streak + the Rybelsus 30-min empty-stomach timer,
   built for the pill, not bolted onto a needle app.
Lead with privacy (the emotional hook), prove it with pill-native depth. "Free" is the
proof, not the pitch — reinforce the privacy stance, don't shout the price.

## Voice
- Lead with a *tension* (privacy, the quiet, the wasted dose), never a feature list.
- One subtle emotional **or** informational beat per reel — never preachy, never a claim.
- Never: fake before/afters, fake people, medical claims, "cure/lose X lbs". Just the routine.
- Pill-native language: "the pill", "daily", "the 30-minute window" — never "shot/injection/site".

## Reel structure (every reel, 1080×1920, ~10–12s, silent — add trending audio in-app)
1. **Hook beat (~3.5s)** — the emotional/informational line over **aesthetic B-roll**
   (pill/water/breakfast/nightstand), not flat gradient. This is what fixes "bland".
2. **App beat (~4s)** — a real **app screenshot** on the brand gradient with a short caption.
   This is the "show the app" fix. Gentle Ken-Burns zoom.
3. **Twist endcard (~3s)** — the signature privacy line + app icon + `@glpillapp · glpillapp.com`.
