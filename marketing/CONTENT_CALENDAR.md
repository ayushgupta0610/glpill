## Rules (from the growth guide)
- 1 quality post/day beats spraying 5. Test formats; when one hits 1,000+ views consistently, make variations.
- CTA in caption + pinned first comment, never a hard sell in the video.
- Two metrics: watch time (hook in 3s) + comments. Short favors both.
- 1–2 trend posts/week on top of these.
- Patience: don't judge before ~100 tries. Double down on winners.
